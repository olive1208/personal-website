## What it is

Bear's Kitchen is a personal cooking journal. You log a dish you made, add a short note, and save it. Each dish gets simple recipe steps a beginner can follow. You can also ask the app for AI recipe ideas when you don't know what to cook next.

## Why I built it

I am new to cooking. I wanted one place to keep track of what I made and what I would change next time. I also wanted recipes written for someone like me, with clear and simple steps.

I called it Bear's Kitchen because we are Cal students. Go Bears.

I built it for fun.

## How it works

- **Log a meal.** Add the dish name and a short note in a few seconds.
- **Browse your journal.** Dishes are saved as pages you can filter by cuisine, such as Chinese, Thai, Italian, Japanese or Mexican.
- **Get recipe ideas.** The app uses AI to turn your notes into friendly, beginner-level recipes.
- **Share.** Each dish has its own page you can send to a friend.
