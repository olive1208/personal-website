My grandma passed away today. She was 93. She had been very weak for over a year, but in the end she left peacefully, without much pain.

She is the first family member I have ever lost. When I heard the news, I couldn't stop crying. The funeral will be in two days. I am so sorry I can't be there to say goodbye.

She was a great woman. She never learned to read, yet she raised five children, and every one of them made it from a small village to the city.

When I was little, we didn't always get along. She was very strict with me. Every time I brought friends home, she scolded me for being too loud. She didn't like that I was so free-spirited and never came home on time. She also didn't like how casual I was with my parents, since she grew up in a traditional family where everyone knew their place. It took us a while to get used to each other's temper.

Still, no matter how loud we were, she always cooked good food for my friends, and she always worried about my safety. Over time, she became a grandma to my closest friends too. When I left home for high school, she started calling me, and she was always so proud of me. She wanted to know my grades and everything I was doing at school, and she would call her sister to tell her all about me.

Grandma, thank you for raising me, for feeding my friends, and for always being proud of me. I miss you. The sky glowed so beautifully at sunset today, just like your life. Rest well.
